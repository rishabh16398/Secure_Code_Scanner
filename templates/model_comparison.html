🧮 How CWE Risk Scores Are Calculated
CWE Risk Score = V × I × L × Vol × C × 100 (capped at 100)
V = Validation
1 if any TP exists, 0 otherwise. Kills the score entirely if no true positive found.
I = Impact (Severity)
CRITICAL=1.0, HIGH/ERROR=0.8, MEDIUM/WARNING=0.6, LOW/INFO=0.4
L = Likelihood
Runs with TP / Total runs. e.g. 7/10 = 0.7
Vol = Volume (Spread)
log₁₀(TP_file_count + 1). Measures how many files are affected.
C = Confidence
1 tool=0.7, 2 tools=0.85, 3+ tools=1.0
Aggregation metrics shown below are all derived from these per-CWE risk scores:
Σ Risk = sum of all CWE scores  |  Σ Risk² = sum of squared scores (amplifies severe CWEs)  |  RMS = √(Σ Risk² / N)  |  Σ √Risk = sum of square-rooted scores (dampens outliers)  |  Geo Mean = geometric mean of scores
📏 Σ Risk (Sum of CWE Scores) — lower = more secure
